Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (https://open-meteo.com/)
API: Historical Weather API, https://archive-api.open-meteo.com/v1/archive
License: CC BY 4.0 (https://open-meteo.com/en/licence)
Underlying data: ECMWF reanalysis (ERA5 and related), Copernicus Climate Change Service.
Request: daily temperature_2m_mean and precipitation_sum, 2015-01-01 to 2024-12-31, timezone UTC, for 67 cities (latitude/longitude in tools/make_data/world_climate.py).
Each city's API response is stored unchanged under "response".
Made by tools/make_data/world_climate.py.
